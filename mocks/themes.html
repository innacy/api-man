API-Man: three theme directions

Why the current UI reads as "AI-made": indigo #6366f1 is Tailwind's default accent, paired with soft tinted pills, 12px rounded cards with pastel status chips, and an Inter font that isn't actually loaded. That's the stock look of generated dashboards.

Each option below fixes that with one committed accent, a real typeface, and its own logo mark (also the browser-tab icon). Every option gets a matching light/dark pair; each is shown here in its main mode.
